\section{Discussion \& Actionable Rules}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Actionable Rules for Medical ML Deployment}
Based on our multi-center empirical findings, we formulate five actionable rules for deploying 3D medical image classifiers:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Rule 1 (Spatial Uniformity):} Always resample heterogeneous multi-site scans to isotropic voxel resolution (.0\,\text{mm}^3$) before volumetric 3D convolution to eliminate spatial aspect-ratio distortion.
    \item \textbf{Rule 2 (Leakage Audit):} Enforce Scanner-Site Stratified Group Cross-Validation in multi-center cohorts. Random splitting leaks scanner signatures, artificially inflating AUROC by $+0.15$.
    \item \textbf{Rule 3 (Dimensional Economy):} Translate the spatial Center-of-Mass of non-air voxels to grid center (.5, 39.5, 39.5$) to standardise spatial placement while preserving the full field-of-view.
    \item \textbf{Rule 4 (3D Regularization):} Combine 3D Mixup ($\alpha=0.2$), Label Smoothing ($\epsilon=0.05$), and 15-view Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) to prevent high-dimensional (,000$-voxel) overfitting under medium cohort sizes ( \approx 10^3$).
    \item \textbf{Rule 5 (Calibration First):} Apply post-hoc Platt scaling (=1.45, b=0.20$) to calibrate raw neural network logits before clinical risk scoring. Platt scaling reduces LogLoss from .2618$ to .2453$ and ECE by \%$ (.0142 \to 0.0061$) without altering relative ROC ranking.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Limitations \& Future Work}
While our framework demonstrates robust generalization across 15 acquisition centers, limitations remain:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Modal Specificity:} Evaluated primarily on 3D volumetric neuroimaging; extension to multi-modal 3D MRI-PET fusion is an active direction.
    \item \textbf{Interpretability:} Integrating 3D Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) to visualize sub-regional voxel attention alongside quantitative anatomical biomarker scores.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a standardized, leakage-free framework for multi-center 3D volumetric medical image classification. By combining spatial standardization (.0\,\text{mm}^3$ isotropic, ^3$ COM grid), site-aware group cross-validation, 3D Deep ResNets with Mixup/TTA, and post-hoc Platt scaling, our approach achieves LogLoss = 0.2453 and AUROC = 0.9610 across ,362$ multi-center scans. Our five actionable deployment rules provide a practical blueprint for developing clinically reliable 3D medical artificial intelligence systems.
