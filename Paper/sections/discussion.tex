\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Why Deeper Architecture Works for 3D Medical Imaging}
\label{sec:why_deeper}

The transition from 2 to 3 residual blocks + downsampling stages adds a third hierarchical level, enabling the network to capture features at three spatial scales that align with striatal anatomy:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Stage 1 (40$^3$):} Local texture, edge detectors, voxel-level intensity patterns
    \item \textbf{Stage 2 (20$^3$):} Striatal sub-region patterns -- putamen vs. caudate, anterior vs. posterior
    \item \textbf{Stage 3 (10$^3$):} Global uptake asymmetry, whole-structure shape, left-right gradients
\end{itemize}

This mirrors the known pathophysiology: DaT-SPECT signal loss begins in posterior putamen, spreads to anterior putamen and caudate, with characteristic left-right asymmetry. The deeper hierarchy naturally captures this progression.

\subsection{Scanner Heterogeneity Robustness}
\label{sec:scanner_robustness}

The largest Config C gains occur on lower-resolution scans (+2.3\% AUROC on 3.90~mm and other). This demonstrates:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The deeper architecture learns resolution-invariant features through Mixup augmentation and multi-scale processing.
    \item Coarse-to-fine alignment (rotation $\pm$5$^\circ$ + translation $\pm$4 voxels) is more effective when the network itself is robust to residual misalignment.
    \item Per-volume z-score normalization prevents scanner-specific intensity distributions from biasing the model.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Calibration for Clinical Decision Support}
\label{sec:cal_clinical_discussion}

The 57\% ECE reduction via Platt scaling is critical for clinical deployment:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Risk stratification ($p > 0.75$):} Neurologist referral decisions require reliable high-confidence estimates
    \item \textbf{Trial enrichment ($p > 0.90$):} Calibrated TP rate increases from 76.3\% to 82.4\%
    \item \textbf{Longitudinal monitoring:} Probability changes over time track disease progression
\end{itemize}

Uncalibrated models exhibit overconfidence (ECE=0.0142), risking false certainty in borderline cases. Our calibrated probabilities maintain AUROC while providing reliable absolute risk estimates.

\subsection{Computational Efficiency on Consumer Hardware}
\label{sec:compute}

Training 1.3M-parameter 3D models on 4GB VRAM required careful engineering:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Gradient accumulation ($\times$3) $\to$ effective batch 24
    \item Mixed precision (AMP) $\to$ 2$\times$ memory reduction
    \item Per-volume loading $\to$ minimal CPU RAM
    \item Early stopping (epoch 60--80) $\to$ avoids overfitting, saves time
    \item $80^3$ crop $\to$ fits in VRAM; 96$^3$ OOM
\end{itemize}

This demonstrates that modern deep 3D CNNs are feasible on consumer hardware with proper engineering.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Single tracer:} All scans use $^{123}$I-FP-CIT (DaTSCAN). Generalization to $^{99m}$Tc-TRODAT-1 or other tracers untested.
    \item \textbf{Binary classification only:} Does not differentiate PD from atypical parkinsonism (MSA, PSP) which also show dopaminergic deficit.
    \item \textbf{No clinical metadata:} Age, sex, disease duration, UPDRS scores could improve performance but were unavailable.
    \item \textbf{Holdout test class shift:} 65\% positive vs 55\% training causes calibration mismatch on small test sets.
    \item \textbf{No external validation:} All results on single dataset; independent multi-center validation needed.
    \item \textbf{Inference time:} 10 models $\times$ 15 TTA = 150 forward passes ($\sim$8 sec/scan on RTX 3050 Ti). Distillation needed for real-time use.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Future Work}
\label{sec:future}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Model distillation:} Compress 10-model ensemble into 1-2 models via knowledge distillation for real-time inference.
    \item \textbf{Multi-task learning:} Joint classification + SBR regression + striatal segmentation for interpretability.
    \item \textbf{Domain adaptation:} Test on external datasets (PPMI, local hospitals) with adaptive normalization.
    \item \textbf{Uncertainty quantification:} Monte Carlo dropout or deep ensembles for epistemic uncertainty.
    \item \textbf{Longitudinal modeling:} Incorporate serial scans for progression prediction.
\end{itemize}
