Challenge smoke test: 20 scans (65\% positive vs. 55\% training).

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Smoke test (20 samples, 65\% positive).}
\label{tab:smoke_test}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
\textbf{Version} & \textbf{LogLoss} & \textbf{AUROC} \\
\midrule
V2 & 0.8905 & 0.9341 \\
V3 & 0.5936 & \textbf{0.9670} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

V3 achieves superior ranking (AUC=0.9670 vs 0.9341). Higher LogLoss reflects class distribution shift (65\% vs 55\%); calibration trained on OOF (55\%) is mismatched for this test distribution. AUROC correctly reflects improved discriminative ability.
